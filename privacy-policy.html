Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 · Last Updated: November 2026

1. Overview & Commitment to Confidentiality

Crescent Moon Consulting Services ("CMCS", "we", "us", or "our") provides high-assurance enterprise software engineering, zero-trust cybersecurity advisory, and mission-critical digital infrastructure services. As a cybersecurity practice, safeguarding confidential information, client intellectual property, and personal data is fundamental to our operating ethos.

2. Information We Collect

We strictly limit the data we collect to what is necessary to evaluate business engagements and communicate with client organizations:

  • Inquiry & Contact Details: Full name, professional email address, corporate phone number, organization name, and service inquiry details submitted through our contact form or direct email correspondence.
  • Technical Engagement Metadata: Non-sensitive logs generated during interaction with our web servers (such as IP address, browser user-agent, and request timestamps) solely for operational telemetry and infrastructure security.
  • Career Applicants: Resumes, employment histories, and contact information voluntarily submitted for employment consideration.

3. How We Use Information

We process collected data exclusively for legitimate enterprise purposes:

  • Responding to technical inquiries and scheduling advisory consultations.
  • Preparing formal proposals, scope-of-work agreements, and technical architecture roadmaps.
  • Maintaining zero-trust perimeter defense and monitoring for unauthorized server access or abuse.
  • Zero Commercial Monetization: We never sell, lease, broker, or share client information or applicant data with third-party advertising networks or external data vendors.

4. Data Security Standards & Encryption

All data transmitted through our web services is protected using modern TLS 1.3 encryption in transit. Internal infrastructure and administrative communications are governed by strict zero-trust access controls, multi-factor authentication, and isolated enterprise mail systems.
