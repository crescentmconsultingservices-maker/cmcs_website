Terms of Engagement

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 · Standard Commercial Terms

1. Engagement Framework

These Terms of Engagement govern client relationships, advisory services, software engineering contracts, and cybersecurity reviews executed by Crescent Moon Consulting Services ("CMCS"). Specific deliverables, milestones, and commercial parameters are documented in individually executed Statements of Work (SOW) or Master Services Agreements (MSA).

2. Intellectual Property & Work-for-Hire

Unless explicitly agreed otherwise in a signed Statement of Work, all custom software, bespoke integrations, deployment configurations, and technical documentation authored specifically for a client upon full settlement of contract fees shall belong exclusively to the client as Work-for-Hire. CMCS retains ownership of pre-existing core libraries, tooling, and proprietary benchmarking utilities.

3. Mutual Confidentiality & Security Auditing

Both parties agree to treat all architectural designs, source code, network configurations, security telemetry, and proprietary business logic as Strictly Confidential. For security assessments or penetration testing, activities are conducted exclusively under strict written authorization within mutually agreed rules of engagement.

4. Professional Standard of Care

CMCS commits to performing all technical services in accordance with recognized industry standards, zero-trust security best practices, and deterministic engineering principles. We deploy senior engineers with direct domain accountability to ensure production-grade reliability and architectural integrity.

5. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

Unless specified otherwise in an executed enterprise MSA, these terms and any disputes arising hereunder shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India, with exclusive jurisdiction resting in the competent courts of Erode / Tamil Nadu, India.
